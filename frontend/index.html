How the System Works

Evidence-based verification using retrieval, semantic ranking and Natural Language Inference.

1
Query Generation
The claim is analysed and transformed into multiple evidence-search queries.
2
Evidence Retrieval
Relevant information is retrieved from available knowledge and scientific sources.
3
Semantic Ranking
BGE retrieval and Cross-Encoder reranking identify the evidence most relevant to the claim.
4
Claim Verification
DeBERTa-based Natural Language Inference and aggregation determine the final verdict.
